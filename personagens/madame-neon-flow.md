# Madame Neon – Prompts para o Flow (vídeo 03)

Imagem de referência aprovada: `madame-neon-referencia.webp`

## Como usar
1. No Flow, use **Ingredients to Video** (ou **Frames to Video**) com a imagem de referência em todos os clipes. É isso que mantém a Madame Neon igual em todos eles.
2. Para cada clipe, cole o **bloco base** e depois a **ação + fala** daquele clipe.
3. Cada clipe tem até 8 s. Se a fala ficar cortada, gere de novo ou encurte a frase.
4. Junte tudo no CapCut, coloque o nome das cartas em letra neon e o texto do gancho.
5. No TikTok, marcar como **conteúdo gerado por IA**.

## Bloco base (colar no início de todo clipe)
```
Vertical 9:16, static camera, medium shot, same character and room as the reference image. Madame Neon: glamorous woman in her early 50s, big voluminous dark curly hair, large lavender-tinted sunglasses, big gold hoop earrings, gold chains and bracelets, shiny purple satin jacket over a dark floral top, long hot-pink nails. She sits at a round table with a leopard print tablecloth, tarot cards, lit candles, two silver vintage boombox radios and a snow globe with a palm tree. Behind her: pink neon sign "Karten 24/7", shelves with old TVs, radios and guitars, window blinds glowing pink and blue. 1980s beach town pawn shop at night, cinematic neon lighting, photorealistic. She speaks German in a warm, slightly raspy, ironic voice. Ambient sound: distant traffic and soft synthwave music. No subtitles, no text on screen.
```

## Clipes

**01 – Gancho**
```
A police siren passes outside and red and blue light sweeps across the blinds. She slowly lowers her sunglasses to the tip of her nose, looks into the camera with a knowing smile and says: "Setz dich, Baby. Ich lege dir drei Karten. Eine davon betrifft genau dich."
```

**02 – Interação**
```
She shuffles the tarot deck with her long pink nails, leans slightly toward the camera and says: "Schreib eine Zahl in die Kommentare: eins, zwei oder drei. Die Karten warten nicht."
```

**03 – Karte 1 (parte 1)**
```
She flips the first card and holds it up to the camera, raising one eyebrow, and says: "Karte eins: Der Wagen. Du willst los, und zwar sofort."
```

**04 – Karte 1 (parte 2)**
```
She puts the card down and wags one finger at the camera, amused, and says: "Aber Baby, erst schauen, dann Gas geben. Diese Woche lohnt sich ein Umweg."
```

**05 – Karte 2 (parte 1)**
```
She flips the second card, looks at it, then laughs and waves her hand dismissively, and says: "Karte zwei: Die Liebenden. Nein, nicht das, was du denkst. Es geht um eine Entscheidung."
```

**06 – Karte 2 (parte 2)**
```
She says: "Zwei Wege, beide sehen gut aus. Hör auf dein Bauchgefühl..." then taps the silver boombox next to her, which crackles with static, and adds with a smirk: "...nicht auf das Radio."
```

**07 – Karte 3 (parte 1)**
```
She flips the third card, smiles genuinely and the candles flicker brighter. She says: "Karte drei: Die Sonne. Endlich mal gute Laune auf meinem Tisch."
```

**08 – Karte 3 (parte 2)**
```
She looks warmly into the camera and says softly: "Irgendwas wird diese Woche leichter. Kein Versprechen, nur ein bisschen Licht zwischen den Palmen."
```

**09 – Auflösung**
```
She points at the camera with a long pink nail, grinning, and says: "Und welche betrifft dich? Die, bei der du gerade gegrinst hast. Ich hab's gesehen."
```

**10 – Abschluss**
```
She pushes her sunglasses back up, leans back in her chair and says: "Schreib mir deine Karte und folge mir. Unterhaltung, keine Vorhersage. Ich hab' schließlich einen Ruf zu verlieren." She blows out one candle.
```

## Dicas
- Se ela mudar de rosto ou de roupa, gere de novo com a referência. Não mude o bloco base.
- Se o Flow trocar o texto da placa neon, dá para cobrir depois no CapCut.
- Não escrever "GTA", "Rockstar" ou "Vice City" nos prompts.
